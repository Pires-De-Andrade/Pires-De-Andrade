# João Guilherme Pires de Andrade

## Bio

I am an undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG) and a research fellow at the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA), in Goiânia, Brazil. At the moment, I work on AI applied to healthcare and on information retrieval systems built with large language models.

## Research:

### Kidney allocation · CEIA/UFG 
> I lead a team on the data and modeling side of the project, which is building a model to predict graft rejection in kidney transplantation. I wrote the project's technical review of the state of the art, and I handle the statistical treatment of the laboratory data. I also contribute to the prediction pipeline over donor and recipient molecular data.

### Industrial process optimization · CEIA/UFG
> I work on a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline over documents from ANEEL, Brazil's electricity regulator, and on the system architecture. I also take part in the group's internal NLP programme, with weekly sessions on the concepts, mathematics and papers behind LLMs and RAG.
